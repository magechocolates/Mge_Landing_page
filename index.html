Why Chocolate, Though?

Nobody has ever said 'I can't wait to take my supplements.' Capsules are a commitment. Protein shakes are a whole thing. But chocolate? Chocolate just happens.

Dark chocolate already does a lot on its own. It is genuinely good for your heart and has anti-inflammatory properties.

Each piece is 10–15g. Low sugar. It fits in your pocket, takes two seconds, and doesn't require a morning routine you'll abandon. That's kind of the whole point.

Not a supplement. Not a snack. Both.
